# Posto di Frontiera — English demo

A co-op investigation game set at a border post in October 2003, in the spirit of *Papers, Please*.
An AI chatbot (ChatGPT works best) plays the travellers: you question them in the chat, check their stories
against the documents, the luggage, the computer archives and the phone, then stamp your verdict.

**Play in the browser:** https://valico-di-grest.github.io/border-post/

- 5 short levels: your first day on the job, from a routine check to a suspicious parcel.
- Solo, or online co-op for 2–3 players (booth, computer, phone), with a voice chat on the side.
- Play with the screen split in two: the chatbot on one half, the game on the other.
- After each level a newspaper tells you what happened next. Your choices carry over.

This is an early demo. At the end of the day there are three quick questions: copying the results and
sharing them where you found the game helps a lot.
